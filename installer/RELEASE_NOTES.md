## Bunnys Audio Separator — by Bunnys Framelab

AI stem separation inside Adobe Premiere Pro (2019 and newer), on Windows and macOS.

### Download
| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `BunnysAudioSeparator-Windows-v….exe` |
| Mac with Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) | `BunnysAudioSeparator-macOS-AppleSilicon-v….pkg` |
| Mac with Intel processor | `BunnysAudioSeparator-macOS-Intel-v….pkg` |

After installing, restart Premiere Pro and open **Window → Extensions → Bunnys Audio Separator**.
The AI model (~110 MB) downloads automatically the first time you separate.

**The installers are not code-signed yet.**
- Windows: if you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.
- macOS: if the package is blocked, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
